### **[0:50 – 1:40] Multi-Turn Trading & On-Chain Swap Execution**
*(Visual cue: Cursor types "swap ETH for USDC" → bot asks for amount → user inputs "0.005" → proposal card appears → cursor clicks "Confirm & Swap" → confirmation receipt renders)*

> *"SwapChat features intelligent multi-turn conversation handling. When we enter an incomplete trade like 'swap ETH for USDC', the AI identifies the missing detail and prompts us for the amount.*
>
> *Once we provide 0.005 ETH, it constructs a complete Trade Proposal card displaying expected output, routing, and slippage.*
>
> *We click 'Confirm & Swap'. The transaction is signed and broadcast directly to the Uniswap V2 router on Sepolia.*
>
> *Once the transaction is mined on-chain, SwapChat renders a verified Trade Receipt card complete with gas metrics and a clickable Etherscan link."*
